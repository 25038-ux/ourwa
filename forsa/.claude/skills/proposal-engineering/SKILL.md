# proposal-engineering

1. Every generated section is classified: EVIDENCE_BACKED, REASONABLE_SYNTHESIS, USER_INPUT_REQUIRED, PLACEHOLDER.
2. AI may draft only from approved company content and linked evidence; it may not invent credentials,
   references, staff or figures.
3. Detect unsupported claims and contradictions before review; red-team the draft.
4. Final submission requires an APPROVED FINAL_SUBMISSION approval; FORSA never submits externally.
