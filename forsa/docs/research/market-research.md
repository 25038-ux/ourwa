## Competitive patterns to learn from (not to clone)
- Capability-based matching with explained scores and risk flags (Tenderlake).
- Tender discovery + company profiles + analysis (Tendly).
- Early procurement intelligence, historical data, buyer intelligence, pre-RFP signals (Deltek GovWin IQ).
- Structured bid/no-bid, compliance matrices, AI-assisted responses (TenderRender, Lucius, TenderAI, BidDNA).
- Trusted content library and response workflows (Loopio).

## FORSA's intended moat
Normalised opportunity history · company capability graph · buyer graph · partner graph · awards/outcomes ·
evidence graph · local FR/AR (later Hassaniya) terminology · learned bid/no-bid outcomes · plan-to-tender
linkage · source lineage · workflow data.

## Mauritania context (to verify)
- The national portal already sends email alerts ⇒ FORSA must not compete on alerts but on *fit, evidence,
  gaps, effort and next action* for a specific company.
- Procurement plans enable pre-tender "early signals".
- Mobile-first usage; WhatsApp may matter more than native apps early on.

## Validation plan (spec §93)
Concierge MVP with 5 companies (IT, construction, solar, engineering, suppliers): build twin, run matching,
deliver daily report, record false positives/negatives and reactions. Only automate what repeatedly creates value.
