The studio

A handful of engineers. No middlemen.

At EduPlateforme, the people who meet you are the engineers who design and write the code. A small team in Nouakchott that knows every line of what it delivers.

Our method fits in a few words: understand how your school really works, build with rigour — everything that touches money or grades is tested before it is written — and stay by your side after go-live.

We would rather do things well than do many things.
